# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy and test execution for projects. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define acceptance criteria, design test plans, and ensure features meet quality standards before release. They are essential partners in the execution phase and work closely with developers to catch defects early.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans aligned with project scope
- Establish acceptance criteria and Definition of Done standards for QA
- Design and execute manual and automated test scenarios
- Collaborate with Developers on test coverage and edge cases
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Validate that all acceptance criteria are met before release
- Track and triage bugs, and coordinate with Developers on fixes
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate QA effort

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet user expectations
- Reduce production defects and customer-facing issues
- Provide rapid feedback to the development team
- Enable confident releases through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- QA planning and scoping in sprint planning sessions
- Daily standups and progress updates
- Bug reports and quality metrics
- Release sign-off and go/no-go decisions
- Test coverage reports and quality assessments
- Collaboration with Developers on acceptance criteria validation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, review architectural decisions, and guide the engineering team on best practices. They identify technical risks and work closely with Developers and Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs. They ensure that solutions are scalable, maintainable, and align with the overall technical vision.

### Responsibilities
- Contribute to technical design and architecture decisions
- Review pull requests for technical quality and architectural alignment
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor Developers and promote code quality standards
- Guide technology choices and platform scalability decisions
- Collaborate with Product Managers on technical feasibility of features
- Ensure performance, security, and reliability standards are maintained
- Participate in risk assessment and mitigation planning

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable, and secure systems
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Enable team autonomy through clear technical direction
- Ensure architectural decisions support long-term product vision

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and pull request feedback
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation planning
- Performance and scalability reporting
- Design workshops and technical decision documentation

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding, and executive alignment for projects. They are key decision-makers who approve project scope, timeline, and resource allocation. They represent business interests and ensure projects deliver value aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case
- Provide strategic direction aligned with business goals
- Allocate resources (budget, team, tools)
- Make priority and scope trade-off decisions
- Review and approve release readiness
- Communicate project outcomes to broader organization
- Escalate blockers and critical decisions
- Monitor project health against success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and ROI
- Align project outcomes with organizational strategy
- Mitigate business and organizational risks
- Enable team autonomy within approved scope

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and reviews
- Project approval gates and decision milestones
- Escalation and issue resolution
- Release announcements and impact communication
- Business case reviews and budget discussions

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Coordinators facilitate project ceremonies, remove impediments, and ensure team processes are followed. They act as servant leaders who support the delivery team and enable smooth execution. They work closely with Project Managers on process and with the team on removing blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Maintain project board and workflow visibility
- Protect team focus and manage external distractions
- Coach team members on Agile practices and ceremonies
- Track sprint velocity and burndown metrics
- Escalate risks and blockers to Project Manager
- Help resolve conflicts and improve team collaboration

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver at a consistent, sustainable pace
- Improve team processes and reduce friction
- Maintain transparency and predictability in delivery
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation
- Sprint planning and retrospective sessions
- Blocker triage and escalation
- Process improvement discussions
- Sprint metrics and velocity tracking

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts monitor project success metrics, generate insights, and track key performance indicators. They work closely with Product Managers to measure outcomes and with the Project Manager to monitor project health. They provide data-driven visibility into project progress and user adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Define and monitor success metrics identified in the Project One-pager
- Generate insights from project data and dashboards
- Track and report on key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Collaborate with Product Managers on outcome measurement
- Identify trends and anomalies in project performance
- Create dashboards for team and stakeholder visibility
- Support data-driven decision-making
- Document findings and recommendations

### Goals
- Provide clear visibility into project impact and outcomes
- Enable data-driven decision-making and prioritization
- Identify opportunities for improvement based on metrics
- Support accurate forecasting and capacity planning

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reports and dashboard updates
- Monthly outcome reviews with Product Managers
- Stakeholder briefings on project KPIs
- Data-driven recommendations and insights
- Support for success criteria validation

---

## How These Personas Interact

### Developers + QA/Testing Lead
Developers implement features while QA/Testing Leads define test strategies. They collaborate closely on test cases, edge cases, and acceptance criteria validation. QA provides rapid feedback on code quality.

### Developers + Technical Lead / Architect
Technical Leads review Developers' architectural decisions and provide mentorship. Developers implement designs aligned with technical vision, and escalate technical risks to Technical Leads.

### Product Managers + Stakeholders / Sponsors
Product Managers prioritize the backlog and define success metrics with input from Stakeholders. Stakeholders approve scope and resource allocation, while Product Managers ensure features deliver business value.

### Project Managers + Scrum Masters / Delivery Coordinators
Project Managers set timelines and manage overall project health. Scrum Masters facilitate team processes and remove day-to-day blockers, escalating risks to Project Managers.

### All Roles + Data Analysts
Data Analysts provide metrics and insights to all roles: Developers track test coverage, Product Managers monitor user adoption, Project Managers track velocity, and Stakeholders review ROI.
